## 🎯 Objective
Learn video marketing and YouTube optimization basics by building a complete content strategy: topics, titles, thumbnails, keywords, descriptions and a publishing schedule for a new channel.

## 🧭 Channel Strategy
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Channel name | AI Build Lab |
| Niche | AI-assisted coding and project building for engineering students |
| Target audience | B.Tech / engineering students in India (18-23) who want portfolio projects, internships and hackathon wins but feel they lack coding experience |
| Language | English with light Hinglish |
| Goal | Reach 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours in 6 months by solving searched problems (tutorials) and building trust (personal build stories) |

### Content pillars
| Pillar | What it covers | Main growth driver |
|---|---|---|
| Build Tutorials | Step-by-step builds of real apps using AI tools | Search traffic |
| Student Career | Portfolio, resume, internships, hackathons | Search + suggested |
| Build Stories | Honest wins and failures from my own projects | Subscribers / trust |

**Why this mix?** Tutorials get found through search (people are already looking for them), career videos get shared among students, and personal build stories turn viewers into subscribers.

## 🔍 Keyword Research Method
1. **YouTube autocomplete** — type a seed term (e.g. *"ai projects for"*) in YouTube search and note the suggestions; these are real searches.
2. **Google Trends** — compare keywords with the filter set to **YouTube Search**, region **India**, last 12 months. Keep terms with steady or rising interest.
3. **Competitor check** — look at top-ranking videos for each keyword: their titles, thumbnails, length and view counts. Find the gap (outdated, too long, too advanced).
4. **Pick one primary keyword per video** and put it at the start of the title, in the first line of the description, in tags, and say it out loud in the first 30 seconds.

## 📊 KPIs — how success is measured
| Metric | Target (first 3 months) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | 5%+ | Tests thumbnail + title |
| Average view duration | 40%+ | Tests content & hook |
| Subscribers | 500 | Audience growth |
| Watch hours | 1,500 | Monetization progress |
| Traffic from YouTube search | 30%+ | Tests keyword strategy |

Review YouTube Studio analytics every Sunday; re-design thumbnails for videos with CTR under 3%.

## 🧠 Key Learnings
- Titles and thumbnails work as a pair — the thumbnail makes people stop, the title tells them why to click.
- Search-based topics bring steady views; personal stories build a loyal audience.
- A fixed publishing schedule matters more than posting a lot.
- Analytics (CTR and retention) show what to fix next.
